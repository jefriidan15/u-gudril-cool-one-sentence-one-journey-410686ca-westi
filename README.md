# One Sentence, One Journey

A complete story about a journey, told in exactly one sentence. An AI judge compares the stories pairwise, and the best one-sentence story wins.

## The story

Amara leaves her mountain village carrying only her grandmother's paper lantern; after three days of pine smoke and rain she knocks on an address memorized as a child, and the woman who opens the door has spent twenty years keeping a room ready for a daughter she had never met — departure, hardship, arrival, and a revelation, all inside a single breath.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the one-sentence story to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
